<section class="section" id="about">
  <h2>About</h2>
  <div class="bio-content">
    <p>I am now a postdoctoral researcher (supervisor: <a href="https://demonlab.org/members/jake">Jacob Vogel</a>) at Lund University, working closely with <a href="https://biofinder.se/">BioFINDER</a>. Before moving to Sweden, I completed my Ph.D. at the National University of Singapore under the supervision of <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/yeolab/thomas">Thomas Yeo</a>.</p>

    <p>My research focuses on <span class="highlight highlight-danger">neurodegenerative diseases</span>, with an emphasis on developing <span class="highlight highlight-primary">machine learning</span> models to analyze <span class="highlight highlight-primary">multiscale, multimodal</span> data, including brain imaging, proteomics, and genomics. My research lines are:</p>
    <ol class="compact-list">
      <li>Automated diagnosis &amp; differential biomarker discovery;</li>
      <li>Mechanism investigation of neurodegeneration &amp; aging;</li>
      <li>Research &amp; clinical toolkit development.</li>
    </ol>

    <p>I have published 20+ peer-reviewed articles in journals including <em>Nature</em>, <em>Nature Medicine</em>, <em>Nature Neuroscience</em>, <em>Science Advances</em>, <em>PNAS</em>, and <em>Medical Image Analysis</em>. My research works can be found at <a class="scholar-citation-badge" href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&amp;user=La_luGsAAAAJ&amp;view_op=list_works&amp;sortby=pubdate"><span class="scholar-citation-label">Google Scholar</span><span class="scholar-citation-count"><span id="total_cit" data-updated="2026-06-29T15:16:28.123118+00:00">934</span> citations</span></a>.</p>
  </div>
</section>
